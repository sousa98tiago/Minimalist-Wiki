The Giraffe is an invisible, interdimensional entity that enters Earth's dimension through the consumption of psychedelics. After establishing a connection with Tomás Fonseca, it begins persistently stalking him, communicating through text messages and gradually interfering with his personal life.

## Appearance

The Giraffe is completely invisible and has never been directly observed. However, it has produced a sketch of itself on a sheet of paper, providing some insight into its physical appearance.

Based on this drawing, the creature appears to be at least as tall as a multi-storey building. It possesses four legs, multiple tentacles, and a pair of large horns protruding from its head. Its legs are covered in fur, suggesting that at least part of its body resembles that of a conventional terrestrial animal.

Its enormous proportions, combined with its ability to remain entirely unseen, make its physical presence particularly unsettling.

## Personality

Upon arriving in Earth's dimension, the Giraffe initially refers to humans as "biological matter", suggesting that it perceives humanity through a fundamentally alien perspective.

After learning to read and write in Portuguese, it develops an increasingly misanthropic worldview. It frequently describes humans as selfish, soulless, and fundamentally flawed, using these beliefs to morally justify the murders of several individuals associated with Tomás.

Despite its hostility towards humanity, the Giraffe develops an intense fascination with Tomás, particularly his poetry. This fixation becomes pathological, to the point that it actively threatens, physically assaults, and emotionally manipulates him to ensure that he continues writing.

The creature's apparent affection for Tomás is deeply contradictory. Although it occasionally communicates in a playful or affectionate manner, its behaviour is fundamentally possessive and coercive. It seems less interested in Tomás' well-being than in maintaining his attention and satisfying its own obsession.

The Giraffe also has a particular fondness for emojis and adopts a distinctly feminine, highly expressive communication style when texting. Its messages are often written in broken Portuguese, with erratic grammar, unusual phrasing, and frequent emoji usage.

## Behavior and Stalking

The Giraffe exhibits persistent stalking behaviour, maintaining an almost constant presence around Tomás without ever making itself visible.

Its primary method of communication is through text messages sent from Tomás' former phone number. These messages are often unsolicited and unpredictable, ranging from seemingly harmless observations to cryptic statements, demands, and direct threats.

The creature demonstrates an unsettling awareness of Tomás' personal life. It appears capable of following his movements, monitoring his interactions, and keeping track of the people he encounters. Its messages suggest that it is familiar with events that Tomás has not shared with anyone, reinforcing the impression that he is being continuously observed.

Rather than immediately resorting to violence, the Giraffe initially establishes itself through psychological intimidation and persistent unwanted contact. It gradually makes its presence known, ensuring that Tomás understands that he is being watched, even when there is no visible indication of the creature's whereabouts.

Its stalking is not limited to surveillance. The Giraffe actively interferes with Tomás' relationships, eliminating individuals it considers harmful or undesirable. These interventions isolate him from the outside world, making the creature an increasingly dominant presence in his life.

The Giraffe's behaviour suggests that it does not simply want to follow Tomás: it wants to become an unavoidable part of his existence.

## Information

Very little is known about the Giraffe's origins. It is unclear whether it originates from another planet, an alternate dimension, or some entirely different form of reality.

Its biological composition and physiology are equally mysterious. There is no information regarding its nutritional requirements, metabolism, health, lifespan, or whether it even experiences conventional biological needs such as hunger, thirst, or fatigue.

The precise nature of its connection to psychedelics also remains unknown, as does the extent of its abilities and whether other entities similar to it exist.
