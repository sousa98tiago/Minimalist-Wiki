"Strange Animals: Invisible Giraffe" is a horror novel written by Tiago Sousa in 2023. The narrative follows a depressed young man stalked by a towering, invisible, and thin entity that systematically eliminates people from his life.

## Overview

The protagonist, **Tomás Fonseca**, is a struggling young adult trying to navigate a stagnant life. With his girlfriend temporarily living abroad, he works as a waiter at his uncle's restaurant. As an outlet for his depression, Tomás channels an intense obsession into poetry, eventually covering the walls of his home with his verses.

---

## Plot Summary

### The Catalyst

Seeking a spiritual breakthrough to pull him out of his mundane routine, Tomás takes a psychedelic mushroom in his car one night.

The morning after, he wakes up at home to discover he has lost his phone. Although he quickly replaces it, he immediately begins receiving bizarre text messages from his old number. Written in erratic, poor grammar, the mysterious sender cryptically remarks that they resemble the giraffe emoji.

### The Killings

From that moment on, the entity possessing the phone—and silently shadowing Tomás—begins violently murdering anyone who mistreats or crosses him:

- **The Uncle:** The restaurant owner is found dead in a brutal, bloody scene.
- **The Recruiter:** A recruiter who rejected Tomás's job application is killed when a block of cement falls from a rooftop construction site, striking her head.
- **The Father:** Tomás has a strained relationship with his father, an engineering business owner who emotionally and physically abuses him (including striking him in the face during an argument in a car). The creature eventually pulls the father from the balcony of his high-rise apartment, staging the fatal fall to look like an accident.

### Conclusion

Seeking another escape, Tomás takes psychedelics once more. He awakens in a dark forest surrounded by scraps of paper featuring a poem written by the Giraffe.

A tentacle suddenly reaches out and hangs him. The novel concludes with the full text of the Giraffe's poem, which heavily implies that death is the ultimate mercy to finally end Tomás's suffering.

## Trivia

- **Format and Design:** The original publication of "Strange Animals: Invisible Giraffe" was printed in color and featured dialogue bubbles representing mobile phone text messages, an effect achieved using specialized LaTeX packages.
- **Anthology Connection:** The original release included a preview for the next segment in the anthology, "Metallic Deer", though that project has been on hiatus for three years.
