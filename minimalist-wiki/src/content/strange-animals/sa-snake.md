Basilisk is a talking snake with the ability of human speech and reasoning.

## Personality

Outwardly, Basilisk presents himself as friendly, polite, and highly agreeable. He is often sarcastic, enjoys telling jokes, and possesses a deep obsession with German philosophy—frequently citing thinkers such as Max Stirner.

Beneath this polite facade, Basilisk is deeply sadistic, cruel, manipulative, Machiavellian, and cunning. He enjoys convincing humans to commit murder purely for fun, relying entirely on his rhetoric and social intelligence to manipulate others to execute his will.

## Information

Despite his uncanny speech, reasoning, and potentially eternal lifespan, Basilisk possesses no other supernatural properties or powers. In his daily existence, he eats rats.

Over time, his deceitful nature and psychological games have earned him numerous epithets, including _The Tempter_, _The Father of Lies_, _The Enemy_, and _The Fallen Star_.
