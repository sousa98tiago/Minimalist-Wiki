Coffee Time! is a cozy pastry shop in Pleasantville, known for its freshly baked treats, warm atmosphere, and friendly service. It is a popular spot among the town's inhabitants, whether they're stopping by for a quick coffee, enjoying an afternoon snack, or catching up with friends.

The shop is also [[char-giraffe|Giraffe's]] workplace, where she spends much of her time serving customers and making sure everyone feels welcome.

## Appearance

Coffee Time! has a welcoming, old-fashioned charm, with a comfortable interior that invites customers to slow down and enjoy their visit. The smell of freshly brewed coffee and baked pastries fills the shop, creating a pleasant atmosphere throughout the day.

Its sign, displaying the shop's name, has seen better days. Some of its letters have faded over time, giving it an unintended alternative name.

## Information

The inhabitants jokingly refer to the shop as "Coffee Tim" because the letter "e" and the exclamation mark have faded from its sign.

Despite the sign's unfortunate condition, nobody seems particularly interested in replacing it. The nickname has become so popular that even regular customers occasionally use it when referring to the establishment.

The shop is a familiar meeting place for the inhabitants of Pleasantville, offering them somewhere to relax, socialize, and enjoy a little treat.

## Trivia

- Opening Hours: Coffee Time! is open from Tuesday to Sunday, between 12 p.m. and 6 p.m.
- Who's Tim?: Newcomers visiting Coffee Time often ask Giraffe who Tim is, assuming he must be the owner or someone who works there. Giraffe usually has to explain that there is no Tim.
- Day Off: The shop is closed on Mondays.
- Local Tradition: Many inhabitants deliberately call it "Coffee Tim", even when they know the shop's actual name.
